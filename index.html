// THE PHILOSOPHY

ONE RUNNER CAN START IT.
A COMMUNITY KEEPS IT MOVING.

Bertusew Running Club was founded on the pavement and trails of Addis Ababa with a clear, unwavering belief: endurance is not just for the elite—it is a foundation for living a disciplined, connected, and healthy life.

Whether you are lacing up shoes for your very first kilometer or preparing for a personal marathon record, you do not have to move alone. We are building a culture anchored in discipline, accessible health, and genuine Ethiopian camaraderie.

01

Beginners First

Zero intimidation. Structured pacing support so you build stamina safely.

02

Recreational Balance

Sweat, unwind from your work week, and connect with positive people.

03

Serious Endurance

High-altitude engine work, distance blocks, and race-day discipline.

You do not have to be fast to belong. You only need the willingness to show up.

TIER 01

BEGINNER

Walk-Run & Gentle Baseline

For people starting their running journey or returning after injury. Focus is on breath regulation, posture, and zero shame.

No runner left behind

EASY / RECREATIONAL

Conversational Aerobic Pace
